Conserv Machinery – Transforming Online Visibility for a Heavy Machinery Dealership

Boosted online visibility for this Bowling Green, KY dealership with SEO and website optimization. Organic keywords more than tripled since September 2024.

Book Your Service Now
Overview

‍

Conserv Machinery, a heavy machinery dealership based in Bowling Green, KY, partnered with us to enhance its online presence, drive organic traffic, and improve conversions. The goal was to create a high-performing, SEO-optimized website that not only attracted potential buyers but also provided a seamless user experience.

Since starting work on the project in September 2024, the website’s organic keyword rankings have more than tripled, leading to a significant boost in online visibility and potential sales.

‍

Challenges

  1. Low Organic Visibility – The website had limited search presence and wasn’t ranking for high-value keywords.
  2. User Experience & Conversion Optimization – The site required structural improvements to increase engagement and drive more leads.
  3. Competitive Industry – The heavy machinery sector is highly competitive, requiring a strategic SEO and content approach to stand out.

‍

Our Approach

1. SEO-Driven Website Optimization

  • Conducted a full SEO audit to identify key areas for improvement.
  • Optimized on-page elements, including title tags, meta descriptions, and headings.
  • Implemented internal linking strategies to improve crawlability and user flow.

2. Keyword & Content Strategy

  • Identified high-intent keywords relevant to the machinery dealership industry.
  • Created SEO-friendly content targeting both buyers and renters of equipment.
  • Developed location-based landing pages to enhance regional visibility.

3. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

  • Improved site structure and navigation to make equipment easier to find.
  • Optimized the contact and inquiry forms to capture more leads.
  • Integrated a loan calculator to help potential buyers assess financing options.

‍

‍

Measurable Results

  • Organic keyword rankings tripled since September 2024.
  • Significant increase in organic traffic without relying on paid ads.
  • Enhanced user experience and site engagement, leading to improved lead generation.

‍

Final Outcome

Conserv Machinery’s website is now a high-performing, SEO-optimized platform designed for long-term growth. By focusing on search visibility, user experience, and conversion strategies, we’ve positioned the business for sustained success in the competitive heavy machinery industry.
